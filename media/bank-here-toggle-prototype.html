Bank here or on the alt: the row's pick button

Items this character banks itself (BH: its crafting still uses them, or you pinned them to bank here) keep their row in the mailbox window, with the same pick button the Me / Friend / Guild rows have. On a BH row its first state is Here, then Alt, then each binding that takes the item. Each click moves it to the next one, and the row moves with it. Click a pick button to try it (hover it for its tooltip). With a controller, use ↑↓ to move and F (Square) to pick.

One button, as today

  • It's the same 56 px pick button the Me / Friend / Guild rows use (Greater Magic Essence shows one for comparison: Me → Guild). It's named for where the item goes now, a click moves it to the next choice, and it's lit (mint) once you've picked something other than the item's own rule.
  • On a BH row, Here takes the place of Me, because the item's own rule there is "bank it here". The cycle is Here → Alt → Friend / Guild → Here (Wool Cloth: Here → Alt → Friend, where Harl's First Aid binding takes it).
  • Here (teal, BH): the item stays in your bags, as it does today.
  • Alt (green, BA): the row moves to Mailing under the bank alt, with a BA bank chip and "your pick".
  • Friend / Guild: the row moves to Mailing under that player, with their "to Harl" chip, like a pick today.
  • Each time, the To: lines, mail count, postage and Send button update. The preview stays exactly what gets sent.
  • The tooltip on the button is today's UI.PickLines: "Where this goes, for this visit", one line per choice with the current one marked, and what a click does. The pick clears at MAIL_CLOSED, as it does now.
  • The bag slot's tag follows the pick: BH, BA or F.

Which rows get it

  • BH rows: what your own professions still use (Valuation.BankForCraft) and bank-here pins. Plus, as today, any row a binding takes (Routes.Choices).
  • Never on a hard keep: a quest item, a conjured item, ammo, a soulbound item, a locked item or a tool. A locked BH row shows its button greyed out, and clicking the row unlocks it first.
  • Alt drops out of the cycle when there's no bank alt, when the bank alt is skipped this visit, and on the bank alt itself. A BH row with no binding then has no button. Wool Cloth keeps Here → Friend. Try the situation buttons above.
  • The 5s reserve still applies: a pick adds postage, and Send greys out if paying it would leave you under 5s.

Mouse, keyboard and controller

  • Mouse: click the pick button. Clicking anywhere else on the row still locks or unlocks the item.
  • Controller: the row is still one D-pad stop, and Square moves the pick (UI.PadPick, unchanged). The highlight follows the item to its new list.
