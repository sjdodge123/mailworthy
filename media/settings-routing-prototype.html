Mail routing, one press from Settings

Today your bindings are three presses deep: Settings, the Bags and more tab, then Friends... (or Friends and guild... while you use none of them), then Add a binding.... The proposal puts a Mail routing... button on the settings' top row, next to the two tabs. It opens one window that lists your bindings, the guild's rules and the bank alt, in the order Mailworthy uses them, with Add a binding... right under your bindings. Click a button to try it, or use ←↑→↓ (D-pad), Enter (Cross) and Esc (Circle).

Today (as built)

  • Settings opens with /mw, the minimap button, or the mailbox window's Settings button, on the tab you last used (Mailing the first time). Its top row has two tabs: Mailing and Bags and more.
  • Bags and more › Windows has Friends (N bindings)..., Guild (rules on)... and Mail ledger.... While you have no binding, no guild template in use and nothing in the ledger (solo, SettingsUI.Solo), those three are one Friends and guild... button.
  • Friends... opens the Friends window ("Send materials to friends") on its list. With no binding of your own it skips the list and opens straight on Add, step 1. Friends and guild... always opens the list, which has Guild... and Mail ledger... on its bottom row.
  • Then Add a binding..., pick a player, pick the profession. That's 3 presses from Settings to step 1 (2 when Friends... skips the list), plus 1 if Settings opened on Mailing (counted above).
  • Outside Settings: /mw friends (or bindings), /mw guild, /mw ledger, and the mailbox window's Friends... by its Bindings heading, which shows only while this visit has binding rows.

The proposal

  • B, recommended: a third button, Mail routing..., on the top row. The tabs narrow from 182 to 120 px to make room. It isn't a tab: it opens the routing window next to Settings, like Locked items.... It's on both tabs, so it's always one press away.
  • The Friends window becomes Mail routing and always opens on its list, which reads in the order Mailworthy routes items (after what this character's own professions keep): 1 your bindings (cap, scope and Remove, as today) with Add a binding... right under them; 2 the guild's rules: whose template, how many rules, Guild rules: on / off on the line, and Guild... for the rules themselves; 3 the bank alt, who gets the rest, with Change... (opens Settings' picker).
  • A: a third tab, Mail routing, inside Settings. At 420 px the binding rows' steppers don't fit, so the tab shows a short read-only list plus Add a binding..., Edit bindings..., the guild line and Mail ledger.... Editing still happens in the routing window, so your bindings would show in two places, and the tab would be a new view to test.
  • Why B: one list of bindings, Settings keeps its size, and the window is the Friends window you already use. To build it: one button in SettingsUI (RoutesUI.Show(frame, "list")) and the guild and bank alt lines in RoutesUI's list view. What gets mailed doesn't change, and no Blizzard UI is touched.

Where Friends, Guild and Mail ledger go

  • Bags and more › Windows keeps Locked items, Pinned items, Bank alt summary and Report: two rows instead of three or four. Friends, Guild, Mail ledger and Friends and guild... move to Mail routing, and the solo fold isn't needed any more.
  • Guild: Mail routing › Guild rules: on / off (1 press) or Guild... (its rules; the guild master's and officers' editor). /mw guild stays.
  • Mail ledger: Mail routing › Mail ledger.... /mw ledger stays.
  • A solo player gets the same button. Its list says "None yet", with Add a binding... under it and the guild and bank alt lines below.
  • The mailbox window's Friends... opens the same window, renamed Routing.... /mw friends and /mw bindings stay, and /mw routing is new. The "Ways in" table under the windows lists every way in, so nothing becomes unreachable.

Mouse, keyboard and controller

  • Every new control is a plain W.Button in a window that already uses Nav, so each is a D-pad stop: Mail routing... (Left / Right along the top row, Down into the tab), and in the routing list Add a binding..., Guild rules: on / off, Guild... and Change.... Cross presses, Circle closes the top window.
  • No right-click, hover-only or typing-only action. A button's tooltip also shows when the highlight is on it (Widgets.Tip), which the bar under the windows imitates.
  • spec/dpad_reach_spec.lua: B adds no view, but three walks change. "settings: Mailing, Bags and more" gets one more stop on each tab, three fewer on Bags and more with a binding, and loses the solo Friends and guild... check. "friends: the list" gets the new stops, and "friends for a solo player" opens from Mail routing.... A would add a view, "settings, Mail routing", which needs a walk of its own.
  • When it's built: docs/ARCHITECTURE.md rows for SettingsUI, RoutesUI and the new /mw routing. The critical path doesn't change (no new way to mail).
