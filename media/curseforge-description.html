Mailworthy clears your bags at the mailbox. One click sends everything worth more on the auction house (or worth more disenchanted) to your bank alt, and leaves the rest in your bags for the vendor. No more checking prices item by item, no more dragging twelve items into a mail at a time.

Made for World of Warcraft: Forever. Works with mouse and keyboard, and with a controller.

How it works

  1. Open any mailbox and click the Mailworthy button under the mail window.
  2. Check the Mailworthy window: who it's going to, what will be mailed and why, and what stays in your bags and why. Click any item to keep it.
  3. Click Send. Mailworthy fills as many mails as it needs (12 stacks each) and sends them all with that one click.
  4. Sell what's left to the nearest vendor: after sending, the items worth more at a vendor (grey junk too) carry a small V tag in your bags. Changed your mind about one? Lock it and the tag goes.

The first time, Mailworthy asks you to pick your bank alt. You choose from the characters you've logged into on this realm and faction, so there are no typos.

What gets mailed

For every item in your bags (all its stacks together, in every bag you carry, reagent bag included), Mailworthy compares what you'd get from a vendor with what it's worth on the auction house after the 5% auction cut, the listing deposit and the postage (30 copper per stack). An item is mailed when it's clearly worth it: by default at least 5 silver and 25% more than the vendor pays. Both numbers are adjustable.

No math needed on your side. The price is the 3-day median of your Auctionator scans, so one undercut can't flip a verdict; half the deposit counts, since the game refunds it on a sale; a flooded market is valued at its floor; and the rule knows your realm's launch day. In launch week, white trade goods with no price yet are mailed anyway. Before the first raids, enchanting materials, herbs and elementals get a B verdict: bank them on your alt, they sell for more later. As the realm ages and gold piles up, the minimum gain grows with it.

Mailworthy never mails:

  • items you've locked;
  • the materials your own professions use (see below);
  • your profession tools (fishing poles, Mining Pick, Blacksmith Hammer, Skinning Knife and the like);
  • gold.

Your professions come first

Mailworthy knows which professions the character has and keeps their materials in the bags, whatever they'd sell for: cloth for a tailor (or for first aid), leather for a leatherworker, metal and stone for a blacksmith or an engineer (plus parts, explosives and devices), herbs for an alchemist, dust, essences and shards for an enchanter, meat and fish for a cook. The window shows them under "staying" with the reason, for example your Tailoring needs it. Gathering professions keep nothing: what you mine, pick or skin is what gets sold. Elemental materials are still mailed; lock them if you want to keep them. The whole rule is one checkbox in the settings, on by default.

Send a profession's materials to a friend

Got a guildmate who enchants, or a friend levelling leatherworking? Bind a profession to them once and Mailworthy mails them every material it uses, every time you press Send, until you remove the binding. Type /mw friends (or press Send materials to friends in the settings), pick the player from your party, your guild or your own characters (online players first, with their professions shown and suggested), then pick the profession. Two clicks, or two presses on the controller.

  • The Mailworthy window lists your bindings above the mail list, each with Skip once (just this visit) and Remove. Items headed to a friend show to Bob instead of a gain.
  • Each binding has a price cap, 5 gold each by default. When the market suddenly wants a material, anything at or above the cap goes to your bank alt instead, and the scan tells you why.
  • Bindings cover all your characters on that realm and faction, or just one; your call per binding.
  • Locked items still stay, and a binding beats "keep my professions' materials" for that profession, since you asked for it.
  • If your friend runs Mailworthy too, the addons tell each other which of their characters craft what, and your mail goes to whichever of their characters has the profession: the friend themself, or their crafter alt. Never their bank alt. The window always names who gets it. (/mw feature comms off stops the messages.)

Optional: Auctionator and Siz DisenchantValue

Mailworthy works on its own, and gets smarter with these two addons installed:

  • Auctionator (recommended) provides the auction house prices from your own scans. With it, Mailworthy weighs every item's real value and only mails what's worth selling.
  • Siz DisenchantValue: when it's installed, Mailworthy also compares the disenchant value of green and blue gear (switched on by default), using its numbers: what the materials are worth at today's Auctionator prices, learned from your own disenchants. Gear that's worth more as enchanting materials goes to your bank alt to disenchant. (If you still have Disenchantry, it switches the comparison on too, but it has no prices of its own.)

Without Auctionator there are no prices to compare, so Mailworthy keeps it simple: it mails every tradeable white (common) item you haven't locked. Grey junk stays for the vendor.

On your bank alt

When you log in to your bank alt, every item Mailworthy sent shows what it's for, worked out again with today's auction prices on that character:

  • a small tag on the bag slot: AH to list on the auction house, B to bank for a later phase, DE to disenchant, or V if it's now better sold to a vendor;
  • a tooltip line with today's price and why it was mailed in the first place, so you can see when the market has moved;
  • a summary of what to list, what to disenchant and what to vendor: the Mailworthy button at a mailbox, or /mw summary.

Lock the items you want to keep

  • Mouse: Alt+click a bag item to lock or unlock it.
  • Controller: press L3 on an item in your bags.
  • Or click the item in the Mailworthy window (mouse or controller).
  • Locked items glow blue, like quest items, while a mailbox is open, so your bags stay clean the rest of the time. When you lock an item elsewhere it glows for a moment to show it took. (A setting keeps the glow on everywhere.)
  • Locks stay wherever you move the item, even after sorting your bags. Mailworthy never mails a locked item.

Controller support

Mailworthy works with WoW Forever's controller interface. At a mailbox, switch to Send Mail (R1) and press Triangle (Y). In the Mailworthy window the D-pad moves between buttons, Cross presses and Circle closes it. The settings window works the same way, wherever you open it.

Settings

  • Minimum gain over the vendor price (silver and percent)
  • Include disenchant value
  • Auction listing length used for the deposit estimate
  • Your bank alt
  • Keep my professions' materials (lists the character's crafting professions and what they use)
  • Send materials to friends: your bindings, each with its price cap and scope
  • Bank alt tags on every character, or only on the bank alt
  • Locked-item glow: at the mailbox only, or always
  • Minimap button

Open them from the Mailworthy window, the minimap button, or type /mw.

Good to know

  • Mail only goes between characters on the same realm and faction, so that's who Mailworthy offers as your bank alt and as friends.
  • Mailworthy doesn't post auctions. It gets the items to your bank alt; you list them there (Auctionator makes that quick).
  • Nothing is sent without your click, nothing happens in combat, and the window always shows who gets each item and why it's mailed or kept.
  • Want the numbers? /mw scan shows the price, deposit, postage and gain behind every decision.
