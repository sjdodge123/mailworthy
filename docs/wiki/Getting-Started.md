# Getting Started

## 1. Install

Install **Mailworthy** from CurseForge (or your addon manager). We strongly recommend **Auctionator** too: it's where Mailworthy gets auction prices. Run an Auctionator scan at the auction house once in a while so the prices stay fresh; Mailworthy reminds you when they're getting old. Use **TradeSkillMaster** instead? That works too. See [[Price Sources]].

## 2. Pick your bank alt (optional)

Your **bank alt** is the character that receives the mail and does the selling. Open the settings (`/mw`, or **Settings** in the Mailworthy window) and press **Choose...** next to Bank alt:

- **Pick from the list:** every character of yours that Mailworthy has seen on this realm and faction.
- **This character is my bank alt:** press this while logged in on the bank alt itself.
- **Or type a name**, for a character Mailworthy hasn't seen yet (for example a bank alt on a **second WoW account**). Press **Use name** twice to confirm.

The bank alt is saved per realm and faction, so all your characters there send to the same place. You can change it any time: Settings → **Change...**

No bank alt? Mailworthy still works: what's worth selling stays in your bags, tagged so you can sell it yourself, and your bindings still mail. See [[Your Bank Alt]].

## 3. Your first mailbox visit

1. Open any mailbox and click the **Send Mail** tab. The Mailworthy window opens beside it. (Or press **Mailworthy** under the mail window.)
2. Read the window. It lists who gets the mail, what goes (and how much more it's worth than vendoring), and what stays (and why). See [[The Mailbox Window]].
3. Not happy with something? Press an item to keep it (that **locks** it, see [[Item Locks]]), or open **Settings** to change the rules.
4. Press **Send**. Mailworthy attaches and sends everything for you, 12 stacks per mail, one mail after another.

Mail only goes out from the **Send Mail** tab. Opened on the Inbox, Send is greyed out and reads **Click Send Mail**; click that tab and the window follows you there.

## 4. On your bank alt

Log in on the bank alt and collect the mail. Each item in your bags gets a small tag that says what to do with it: **AH** (list it), **BH** (bank it here), **DE** (disenchant it) or **V** (vendor it). See [[Your Bank Alt]]. Using Baganator, Bagnon, ArkInventory or BetterBags? The tags show there too: see [[Bag Addons]].

Want something back on a main? Press **Return to sender** in the bank alt's Mailworthy window: see [[Return to Sender]].

## Using a second WoW account for the bank alt?

Mail works across accounts, but saved settings don't. On your main, type the alt's name (step 2). On the alt's account, install Mailworthy and Auctionator, run an Auctionator scan there, open `/mw`, press **Choose...** and then **This character is my bank alt**. Each account keeps its own prices.
