# Mailworthy

**Open a mailbox, click Send Mail, press Send, and everything in your bags that's worth more on the auction house than at a vendor goes to your bank alt.** The rest stays in your bags so you can sell it to the next vendor you pass.

Mailworthy is an addon for **WoW Forever**. It's for anyone who levels with full bags and dreads sorting them: which greens to keep, which cloth is worth listing, which herbs to hold.

## What it does, at a glance

| You do | Mailworthy does |
|---|---|
| Open a mailbox and click **Send Mail** | Opens beside the mail window, looks at every bag (reagent bag too) and works out, item by item, where it's worth the most |
| Read the window | Shows **who** gets mail, **what** goes and **why**, what **stays** and why, and how much more you'll make than vendoring |
| Press **Send** | Sends it all in one press: 12 stacks per mail, no gold ever attached |
| Hover an item with the window open | Says where it goes, or why it stays |
| Log in on your bank alt | Tags each item: list it on the AH, bank it, disenchant it, or vendor it, each in its own colour |
| Ctrl+Alt+click an item | Pins it: always mailed to your bank alt, or always banked on this character; again, always vendored |
| Open a merchant | **Mailworthy: sell V** sells the V-tagged items, 12 stacks a press (Buyback can undo it) |
| Bind a profession to a friend | Sends them your cloth, dust, herbs or recipes every time, as long as they still need them |
| Turn on your guild's template | Sends each profession's materials to the guildmate your guild master picked; one press turns it off |
| Press **Return to sender** | Puts everything someone mailed you on the list, addressed back to them |
| Open the mail ledger | Shows every mail sent and received, and who you owe a pay-back |

## Works best with

- **[Auctionator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctionator)**: the prices Mailworthy uses. **TradeSkillMaster** works too. Without either, Mailworthy still works but only knows "white items and staple trade goods sell". See [[Price Sources]].
- **[Siz DisenchantValue](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/siz-disenchantvalue)** or **[Disenchantry](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/disenchantry)**: optional, for disenchant values of green, blue and purple gear.
- **Baganator, Bagnon, ArkInventory or BetterBags**: optional; Mailworthy's tags and lock glow show in them. See [[Bag Addons]].

Mailworthy is free, open source, and on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1743661).
