# Item Locks

A lock means **"never mail this"**. It's the simplest way to keep a rare drop you're saving, gear for an alt, or that Essence of Water you want to use yourself.

## Locking and unlocking

| How | Mouse / keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| In your bags | **Alt+click** the item | **L3** on the item |
| In the Mailworthy window | click its row | highlight the row, press **Cross** |

Do the same again to unlock. `/mw locks` lists this character's locked items.

## How locks behave

- A lock is for **the item**, not the bag slot: move it, split it, stack it, and it stays locked. Locking one stack of Linen Cloth keeps all your Linen Cloth.
- Locks are per character.
- Locked items **glow blue** in your bags while the Mailworthy window is open, and are listed first under Staying. Away from it, a fresh lock glows for a moment so you can see it worked. Want the glow all the time? Settings → **Lock glow: always**.
- Hover a glowing item: the tooltip says it's locked in Mailworthy and how to unlock it.
- The glow shows in Baganator, Bagnon, ArkInventory, BetterBags and on Auctionator's Selling tab too: see [[Bag Addons]].
- Nothing overrides a lock: not the price, not a friend's binding, not your guild's rules, not [[Return to Sender]], not a [[pin|Pins]].
- Want an item **always banked** instead of always kept? Pin it: see [[Pins]].
